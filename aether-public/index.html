Can this be scientifically valid, technically practical and commercially meaningful?

That is the question Aether exists to answer, for a modular algae-based bioreactor aimed at real-world environmental applications.

Right now it is an R&D hypothesis. We have not proven the science, the engineering or the economics yet.

We would rather say that upfront. Anyone who funds or joins Aether at this stage should know exactly what stage it is.
