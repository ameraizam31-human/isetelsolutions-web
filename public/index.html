How long does a build take?

A prototype is typically ready in 2 to 4 weeks. Larger systems with several integrations usually take 6 to 12 weeks. You see progress along the way.

How is our data handled?

We agree on it before any work starts: what is stored, where, and who can see it. Options include running open models on your own server so the data never leaves it, and using providers that do not retain your data. For personal data we design with Malaysia's PDPA in mind.

Which languages can the AI handle?

English and Bahasa Malaysia, including the mixed everyday language people actually type. Other languages are possible depending on the model, and we test with your real examples before committing.

What do you need from us to start?

A short description of the problem, a few examples of the documents or data involved, and one person who can answer questions.
